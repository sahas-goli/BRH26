The public site is https://sash.select. Judges open that hostname for the home page, `/text`, and `/live`. Photon builds every personal `/h` link from `PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, and the demo notes set that to `https://sash.select`, so an iMessage and the website share one origin. `sash.select` is one of the names the GoDaddy Registry search on tech.study marked available, in `submission/domain-shortlist.md`. The live site is that name. A phone can open it with no login.
