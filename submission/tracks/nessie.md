A holder on `/h` can choose Demo bank account as their rail, next to a Solana wallet. `web/lib/nessie.ts` opens a checking account and an escrow account, then transfers the cap from checking into escrow. On poster confirm, `payFromEscrow` sends each bank-rail share and the Solana-rail treasury leg out of escrow, then returns the remainder to checking. On expiry, `refundEscrow` sends the escrow back to checking. The Balances (Nessie) panel on `/live` shows checking, escrow, and status. The depth-0 poster is not paid.
