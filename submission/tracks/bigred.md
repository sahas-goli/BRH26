Six Hops is the navigation. A search for someone you cannot look up moves one friend at a time, and each person uses only what they already know to choose the next link. We never copy a contact graph. The hint card is attributes only: a city, a field, a skill. Fan-out stops at 3 and depth stops at 8, both in `web/lib/types.ts`. Each passer is paid `floor(B/16)`, the match gets the rest, and the poster takes no share. sash.select/live shows the cascade for replay `r_0000`: 764 holders, depth through 8.
