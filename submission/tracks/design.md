The visual direction is `design/poster-direction.md`. A forwarded link is a wanted card: the line FIND SOMEONE WHO, attribute chips, no name and no face, and Pass it to someone closer. The same layout is the link preview, including the static fallback. On a phone the path is `/post`, then the personal `/h` page, then This is me. On the projector, `/live` is the cascade, the branching-factor dial, and the attack slider. On that dial, branching under 2 is red, under 2.5 is amber, and 2.5 or higher is green. The slider sets honest depth and fake hops, then prints our gain next to the MIT gain, with the poster total unchanged.
