# Theme answer card (AL-13)

These three headings are ideation prompts, not prize tracks. The judged theme track is BigRed. Say these if a judge asks how Six Hops is Navigation. They follow the hop chain on https://brh26-brh-26.vercel.app and in `submission/pitch-2min.md`.

## Wayfinding

A poster puts out a hint card: skills and experience, city level, no names and no street addresses. Each holder forwards that card to at most three people, and a recipient at depth eight cannot forward. The person opts in, and the poster confirms before anyone is paid. We do not claim a map, a haptic guide, or an audio wayfinder.

## Exploration and Discovery

The search is for someone you cannot look up. Friends pass the same hint card closer, at most three people each, and the chain stops at eight hops. What turns up is an introduction the person opts into, and the poster confirms before the chain is paid. We do not claim a map, a haptic guide, or an audio wayfinder.

## Accessibility

The target opts in, and the hint stays attributes only: city-level skills and experience, never a name or a street address. That is what the product already does. We do not claim a screen reader, a haptic guide, or an audio wayfinder.
