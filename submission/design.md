# Design track

## Short description

Mobile-first hop and claim pages; full-screen `/live` cascade (d3), branching gauge, attack slider, and Imagine wanted cards as share previews.

## Why Design specifically

1. One job per surface: post, hop, claim, live—no dashboard clutter in the hero flows.
2. `/live` is the room screen; phones get the personal links.
3. Wanted-card OG images make every iMessage/SMS preview on-brand.

## Where in the code

- `web/app/live/`, `web/app/h/[token]/`, `web/app/claim/[token]/`, `web/app/post/`, `/api/og`

## Demo look-at

`/live` on the projector; hop page on a phone.
