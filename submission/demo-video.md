# Six Hops — demo video

A two-minute video for Devpost. Film the live site at [sash.select](https://sash.select). One person can shoot it. Do not submit Devpost from this file. When the video is uploaded, paste the link into the Devpost video field yourself.

The finished file should be **2:00 or under**. Unlisted YouTube is enough.

## Before you record

1. Leave this laptop awake with the Photon agent running. If texts get no reply, start it again from `agent/` with `PHOTON_ENABLED=true` and `PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://sash.select`.
2. Phone A is the poster. Phantom on that phone is set to **Solana Devnet** and can send at least 0.1 SOL.
3. Phone B is the match. It only needs a browser.
4. On the laptop, open [sash.select/live](https://sash.select/live) in a full window. Hide bookmarks. That board is the replay `r_0000`.
5. On Phone A, open [sash.select/text](https://sash.select/text) once and confirm you already have a Photon line. Do not start a search yet.
6. Do one silent rehearsal of steps 3 through 6 below. Then start the recording.

Record the laptop with **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar). Stop with the same keys. Record the phone with iOS Control Center → Screen Recording. You will cut the two recordings together.

Have this tab ready and do not show it until the end, in case the fresh payout is slow: [the 0.5 SOL payout already on devnet](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/29b8QgRSUcSdpmnwiLGKQXHkKLPEemcVPf5ygX8LvnmP9t9EDpmfd8qoxohCkPNw44baqwYafRDRZaMDfZZdDZsK?cluster=devnet).

## What to say

Read this while the pictures change. It is about 280 words, which fits in two minutes if you do not pause.

> Six Hops pays a friend-to-friend search. The person you need is usually a few hops into someone else's contacts. You describe them with attributes only. A friend passes one personal link. The match opts in. The poster accepts, and one Solana transaction pays the route.
>
> Each person who passed the link gets one sixteenth. The match gets the rest. The deposit is the whole reward. A fake extra hop does not raise the match's take.
>
> Here is a real search. Photon asks who we want and how long to look. We send devnet SOL to the address it gives us. That deposit is the reward. The match opens their link, taps This is me, and writes one sentence. The poster accepts. The chain is paid from that search's own wallet. Explorer shows one transaction.
>
> The board behind this is a replay of 764 people, eight hops deep. The same split is what those numbers use.
