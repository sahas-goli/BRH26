# BigRed (main theme: Navigation)

## Short description

Decentralized social search: humans navigate their private contact graphs with local knowledge (Kleinberg / Milgram), and the successful chain is paid correctly.

## Why BigRed / Navigation specifically

1. Theme fit: navigation through a social network, not a map app bolted on.
2. Grounded in the keynote speaker’s navigability results—local routing, no central graph.
3. Venue overnight game makes the abstract theory playable.
