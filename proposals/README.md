## Round 1 finalists (researched 2026-10-03)

The 21 brainstormed ideas were merged and cut down to 8. Each of the 8 got two originality searches (products; research and hackathons) and a skeptical verdict, and was then scored by a 3-judge panel: a BigRed judge, a sponsor panel, and a hackathon veteran. The panel scored the specs *before* the writers fixed the flaws it raised, so these numbers are rough priorities, not predictions.

| Proposal | One line | Panel E (expected wins) | BigRed /10 | Best tracks (panel avg p_win) | Originality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Deep Field Radio](deep-field-radio.md) | 3D-printed radio: a Topic dial and a Year dial; answers only from astro-ph papers published by the dial year, and a verifier catches LLM "future leaks" | 1.07 | 6.7 | SpaceX .33, Tiger Data .23, Hardware .15, Design .13 | Qualified |
| [Halflife](halflife.md) | iMessage venue guide that refuses expired facts, sends a nearby attendee on a 20-second check, then texts you the answer | 1.01 | 6.7 | Tiger Data .29, Photon .27, BigRed .11 | Qualified |
| [Starfix (nogps.club)](starfix-nogps-club.md) | Handheld that gets a position fix from starlight, gravity and a clock, then catches a spoofed GPS feed | 0.95 | **7.7** | SpaceX .31, Hardware .25, BigRed .16 | Qualified |
| [Preflight](preflight.md) | Rehearse a real bus trip; Presage + squeeze stone find *why* a step is hard; the real plan changes | 0.92 | 6.0 | Presage .34, ElevenLabs .14, Design .11 | Qualified |

**Read:**
- **Starfix** has the best shot at BigRed, which is the prize that matters most. It also has the strongest Hardware story and the clearest demo: the judge hides a city and the stars find it.
- **Deep Field Radio** has the highest expected number of track wins, but its theme fit is metaphorical, so BigRed is riskier.
- **Halflife** is the only finalist with real traction, since attendees use it overnight. It overlaps RouteRepair's space, so pitch one or the other.
- **Preflight** rides the least-crowded sponsor track (Presage). It is gated on the SDK streaming by hour two.
- Every finalist can also enter GoDaddy, Software and People's Choice at near-zero cost.
