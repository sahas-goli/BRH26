# Capital One: Best Use of Nessie (sponsor)

- **Sponsor:** Capital One (flagship, $6,500 listed).
- **Winners:** 1
- **Prize:** $250 cash (Devpost).

## Challenge
Reimagine the banking experience by building an innovative app that uses Capital One's Hackathon API, **Nessie** (http://api.nessieisreal.com). Nessie gives mock financial data: accounts, merchants, bills, peer-to-peer transactions. Creatively integrate Nessie's endpoints to empower users and improve their financial lives.

## Requirements
Use the Nessie API. No other technical requirements listed.

## Judging
Innovative financial application. Specific rubric not published.
