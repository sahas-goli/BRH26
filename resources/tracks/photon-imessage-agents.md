- **Sponsor:** Photon (partner tier). Source: https://somethingreat.notion.site/Hackathon-Guidelines-for-Photon-3dce443f36bb803696daf4135f063cd1 (extracted via Notion API; also on the opening slides). Notion also links an "Examples" subpage (not captured).
- **Winners:** 1st + runner-up
- **Prize (per team):**
  - 1st ($700): $400 cash + $300 Photon credits + fast-track to the final interview round with the Photon team
  - Runner-up ($300): $200 cash + $100 Photon credits

## Pitch
A track for builders who see AI coexisting with us: not a utility tool but a being that lives with us, attuned to context, trusted to act with taste. Build agents that participate, not just assist.

## What you can build
- Agents that join human-to-human conversations to suggest, support, or participate
- AI companions that understand social context, emotion, tone
- Multi-agent systems collaborating with humans and each other
- Agents that exist across interfaces: chat, voice, ambient

## Requirement
**Must integrate with Spectrum**, Photon's open-source framework to bring agents to all platforms. Connect the agent to iMessage via the dashboard.

## Judging
- Vision: pushes the frontier of hybrid intelligence?
- Craft: cohesive, responsive, tastefully integrated?
- Depth: handles real-world context and edge cases?
- Traction: could run with real users tomorrow?

What great looks like: real human-in-the-loop collaboration (not a prompt demo reel); context that persists across time, channels, teammates; a delightful default that doesn't demand attention; safety and fallback behavior that earns trust.
