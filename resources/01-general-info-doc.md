## Submission (Devpost, Sun 8:30 AM)
Allow 5+ minutes. Required:
- GitHub or Google Drive link to the whole project.
- Team deck: **Google Drive link to a PDF** (name + members, problem and why it matters, solution + demo screenshots, tech stack, impact/future).
- Devpost write-up: Inspiration, What it does, How we built it, Challenges, Accomplishments, What we learned, What's next.
- Tick every track you are entering ([tracks/](tracks/README.md)).

## Judging
- **Prelim, 9:00 AM, PSB Atrium:** science-fair style, 4 min per team (2 pitch + 2 Q&A), 2-4 judges visit your table.
- **People's Choice:** public Devpost upvotes open after 8:30 AM.
- **Finalists, 11:30 AM, Baker 200:** top 5 BigRed teams re-present (4 min + 2 min Q&A). Not being called does NOT mean you lost: side-track winners are decided in prelims and announced at closing.
