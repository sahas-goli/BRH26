# Past BigRed//Hacks winners

Scraped 2026-10-02 from each year's Devpost gallery (`<event>.devpost.com/project-gallery?winner=true`) plus individual project pages. Event links: [03-devpost-and-website.md](03-devpost-and-website.md). Team names omitted; open the Devpost link.

**Reliability:** the winner-filtered gallery lists real winners first, then pads with non-winners, so I kept only projects confirmed by a winner label or a project page. Prize names come from page-fetch summaries (Devpost blocks direct downloads). Where two reads disagreed, both are shown. Treat prize names for 2018-2022 as approximate.

## Patterns worth using
- BigRed track winners 2024-2025 were polished, demo-able consumer apps with a clear audience: music (3 of 6), scheduling/health, learning; webcam/hand-tracking (wubwub, Road Rage) and Gemini show up repeatedly.
- Hardware, Beginner and Design winners are often distinct small teams, so side tracks are less contested than BigRed.
- Past MLH bounties winners are usually the best use of the API, not necessarily top overall (2025: Bear Escape took Design and Gemini).
- Navigation-adjacent past winners: Pathway (accessibility map, 2024 Beginner), Inneroute (weather-aware routing, 2021), RoomVision (indoor wayfinding for visually impaired, 2019), PathAngel (safe walking paths, 2019), Onyx and Wayer (2015).
